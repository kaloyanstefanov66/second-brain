# Zettelkasten Method

Niklas Luhmann's slip-box: one idea per note, every note gets a unique ID, and notes link to each other rather than living in folders. Over decades the network of links becomes a thinking partner that surprises you.

Principles:
1. Atomic notes — one idea, written in your own words.
2. Link generously; a note without links is an orphan.
3. Structure notes (maps of content) emerge bottom-up instead of being imposed top-down.

The value is in unexpected connections between distant ideas. #pkm
