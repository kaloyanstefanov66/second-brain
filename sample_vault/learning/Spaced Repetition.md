# Spaced Repetition

Reviewing material at **increasing intervals** beats cramming. Each successful recall makes the memory more durable, so the next review can wait longer. The forgetting curve (Ebbinghaus) flattens with every well-timed review.

## How I use it
- Anki for vocabulary and formulas; ~20 minutes every morning.
- New cards: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 3 weeks, 2 months.
- If a card lapses, reset the interval but keep the ease factor.

## Why it works
Retrieval is effortful, and the *effort* of recalling something just before you forget it is what strengthens it. Too-frequent review wastes time; too-sparse review means relearning from scratch. The sweet spot is desirable difficulty.
