# Beekeeping Log

## April 12
Hive 2 is building queen cells — they are preparing to swarm. Added a super to give them space.

## April 20
Hive 2 swarmed anyway. The swarm clustered on the apple tree for two days. Fascinating to watch the scouts: each scout bee finds a potential nest site and performs a waggle dance to advertise it. Better sites get longer, more enthusiastic dances, so more scouts go and check them.

## April 22
The decision is made by **quorum sensing**: when enough scouts (roughly 20–30) are present at one candidate site at the same time, they switch from recruiting to "piping", which tells the whole swarm to warm up and fly. No single bee knows the whole picture; the colony still almost always picks the best site. Source: Seeley, *Honeybee Democracy*.

## May 3
Caught the swarm in a bait box. Calm, strong colony. Varroa count low.
