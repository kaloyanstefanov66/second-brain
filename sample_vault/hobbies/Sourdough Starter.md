# Sourdough Starter

Feeding ratio 1:5:5 (starter : flour : water) twice a day at room temperature.

The yeast and lactic acid bacteria population **doubles** every few hours after a feed, then crashes once the food runs out — the jar rises, peaks, and falls. Bake at the peak. A warmer kitchen speeds the whole cycle up; the fridge slows it to once a week.

If the starter smells like acetone it's hungry — feed it more often or use a higher ratio. Rye flour gives the microbes more nutrients and wakes a sluggish starter.
