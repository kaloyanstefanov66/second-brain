# Running Training Plan (half marathon)

Weeks 1–4 build base mileage at easy pace. Increase weekly volume by no more than 10%.

Progressive overload only works with recovery: the adaptation happens *after* the stress, during rest. Schedule a down week every fourth week. Hard sessions spaced too closely lead to overtraining; spaced too far apart and fitness decays before the next stimulus.

Long run on Sundays, intervals on Tuesdays, everything else easy.
